**Zicoda Space** is an interactive 3D spacecraft engineering simulator and educational mission design game developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**. It empowers users to architect interplanetary missions to **The Moon**, **Mars**, and **Europa (Jupiter)** while balancing real-world aerospace physics—mass budgets, solar inverse-square power attenuation, deep-space communications, and radiation shielding—guided by an expressive, cursor-tracking AI robot companion (**ZICODA-BOT**).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Real-Time 3D Digital Twin & Swappable Launch Vehicles (`Three.js`)
* **Dynamic 3D Spacecraft Assembly:** Every subsystem choice updates the 3D spacecraft hull in real time—unfolding emerald solar wings, mounting glowing Plutonium-238 RTG nuclear batteries, attaching Rose-Gold high-gain deep-space dishes, or adding purple science payload orbs.
* **Rotating 3D Launch Vehicles:** Switch seamlessly between a sleek **Falcon-Class Light Launcher** ($1,250\text{ kg}$ capacity) and a **Heavy SLS Rocket with Twin Solid Rocket Boosters** ($2,800\text{ kg}$ capacity).

### 2. Expressive Floating AI Companion (`ZICODA-BOT`)
* **Cursor-Tracking Physics:** An autonomous floating AI assistant smoothly follows the user's cursor across the mission console, delivering contextual engineering feedback and physics guidance in real time.
* **Emotional & Idle State Machine:** Reacts with emerald smiles when engineering constraints are satisfied, shifts to red warning states with diagnostic alerts upon budget/mass/power violations, and exhibits four distinct idle behaviors (yawning, looking around, sighing, and zero-gravity flips) when the user pauses.

### 3. Multi-Stage Cinematic Mission Flight Sequence
Launching a mission triggers an animated four-phase orbital sequence:
1. **Liftoff & Booster Separation:** Main thruster ignition, camera vibration, and lower-stage booster separation.
2. **Deep Space Cruise:** Interplanetary transit through a dynamic starfield toward the target body.
3. **Planetary Landing & Surface Science Scan:** Touchdown on the target surface and deployment of a wireframe green laser science scanner.
4. **Ascent & Earth Return:** Surface liftoff and return trajectory back to Earth orbit before generating the final telemetry report.

## 🧮 Aerospace Physics & Trade-Off Model

| Target Destination | Solar Efficiency | Required Communications | Required Shielding | Recommended Power |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Moon** | $100\%$ | Standard UHF or Deep Dish | Standard Thermal Coating | Standard Solar ($+350\text{W}$) |
| **Mars** | $65\%$ | High-Gain Deep Space Dish | Reinforced Heat Shield | Enhanced Dual Solar ($+520\text{W}$) |
| **Europa (Jupiter)** | $15\%$ | High-Gain Deep Space Dish | Reinforced Radiation Shield | RTG Nuclear Battery ($+600\text{W}$) |
